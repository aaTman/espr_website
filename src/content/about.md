Hi! My name is Taylor. I'm a co-founder of [Extreme Earth](https://extremeearth.org) where we are working on using AI responsibly and transparently to improve communication and outcomes of extreme weather hazards. I'm a proverbial Swiss-army knife, with professional experience in software engineering, AI model development, data science, research, and getting projects out the door. Non-professionally, I'm ALSO a Swiss-army knife, working on graphic design for almost 20 years (back when forum signatures were cool on IPB boards!), dabbling in recycling older hardware for home server shenanigans, spending some time learning Japanese (definitely cannot pass N5 yet), serving as chair of my local public housing authority board, and enjoying loud, screaming music that is better suited for an angsty 2000's teenager car soundtrack.

I'm currently working with Amy McGovern and NOAA on updating [FrontFinder](https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/aies/4/1/AIES-D-24-0043.1.xml?tab_body=pdf), a convolutional neural net that provides probabilistic information on baroclinic boundaries. I am the main developer of [ExtremeWeatherBench](https://github.com/brightbandtech/ExtremeWeatherBench/), a flexible python library built to improve accessibility of evaluation and benchmarking for high-impact environmental events.
